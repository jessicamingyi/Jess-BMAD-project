Direction 1 · Vintage Postcard

The whole game is a stack of old correspondence: warm paper, double-ruled serifs, perforated stamps and faded postmarks, so every puzzle already feels like something you could slip into an envelope. Givens are set in bold upright type and your own entries in italic ink blue, like handwriting on a printed card. It trades some density for ornament — best if the Postcard reward is the emotional centre of the product.
