Colour Themes · Vintage Postcard, five registers

Same personality throughout - warm paper texture, Georgia serif with small caps, perforated stamp and postmark, givens bold upright, your digits in italic ink. Only the palette changes. Each palette shows daylight and lamplight (dark) side by side; all pairs are measured and the hex values and ratios are recorded in the style comments.
