# Addendum: Traveller Sudoku brief

Context kept for later documents (PRD, UX, architecture). It doesn't belong in the brief itself.

## Kinds of stakes considered (2026-10-05)

The creator's root cause of boredom was **"no stakes or goal"**. It was *not* repetition, grinding or getting stuck. Five kinds of stakes were considered:

| Option | Status | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| A. Mastery path (unlock techniques) | Not chosen as the main hook | Lives on through hints that teach. It could later shape how regions unlock. |
| B. Beat yourself (personal bests) | Parked | Research shows players value stats quietly. A candidate for after the MVP. |
| C. Beat others (daily puzzle, sharing, leaderboards) | Parked | Research shows interest in social play is niche for Sudoku. |
| **D. Journey or collection** | **Chosen** | Flavour: **collection**. Theme: **postcards from places**. |
| E. Risk and pressure (lives, timers) | Rejected | Conflicts with calm play. Research shows the "three mistakes and you're out" rule is a common complaint. |

Within journey, collection was chosen over a learning journey and a story/world. Collection themes considered: garden, creatures, mosaic, **postcards (chosen)**, gems.

## Design guardrails from research (for UX and PRD)

- No interruptions during a puzzle. Reward moments come *after* a solve and stay quiet. No flashing and no loud sound.
- Never "game over". Mistakes may be highlighted (optional), but never end the game.
- Assists can each be switched on or off. Offer number-first input as well as cell-first.
- Never lose progress. Players treat history and collections as possessions, and losing them leads to angry reviews.
- Streaks, if ever added, must be opt-in or forgiving.
