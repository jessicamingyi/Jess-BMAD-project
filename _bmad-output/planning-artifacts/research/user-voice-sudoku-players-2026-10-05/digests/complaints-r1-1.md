## Verdict per hypothesised need

- **N1 uninterrupted / ad-free play:** **Supported** (C1–C8). This is the most consistent complaint, seen in App Store reviews (C1–C4), on Hacker News (C5, C8), and in the flood of "No Ads" apps (C6). Under the two-community rule it is **the top complaint for free apps** (App Store and HN are independent). Caveats: the ranking comes from one aggregator (C4), and most dated quotes are from before April 2025.
- **N2 fair quality puzzles:** **Supported** (C11–C15). Wrong difficulty labels (including "hard" being too easy) appear across App Store, Threads and HN. Forced guessing and non-unique puzzles are each seen only in single sources (C13, C15), so those parts are only partly supported.
- **N3 hints that teach:** **Partly supported** (C16, C17). The complaint about Sudoku.com's confusing hints and the praise for Good Sudoku's teaching hints both come from App Store reviews only. There is no second community.
- **N7 fair pricing:** **Partly supported** (C5, C7, C9, C10). People are willing to pay a small one-time fee (HN). The $5 ad removal is seen as expensive next to a free workaround (low confidence). There is indirect evidence of "paid and still see ads" at Brainium. **No evidence was found of subscription complaints** for these specific apps; Sudoku.com and Good Sudoku are both one-time purchases.

## New needs discovered (not in N1–N8)

- **Forgiving input / no harsh lose-state:** the three-mistake limit plus mis-taps leads to a lost game (C18). JustUseApp ranks it #2.
- **Reliable offline play:** paid apps are expected to work on planes (C19). "Offline" is also in many competitor app names (C6).
- **Respect for time / no grind:** event length (C20); "no energy bars" used as a selling point (C6).
- **Input precision and notes control:** button spacing, lag, reset, selective note deletion, number-first entry mode (C21).
- **Privacy / no tracking:** this came up on HN (C5, C8), where people asked whether the ad provider collects data. Seen only in that community.
