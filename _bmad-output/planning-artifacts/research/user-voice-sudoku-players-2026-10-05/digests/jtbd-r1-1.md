Accessed: 2026-10-05. Run: r1-1. Tool calls used: ~13.

**Method notes and caveats (read first)**
- Reddit could not be fetched (WebFetch blocked for reddit.com) and web search did not surface Reddit threads. Google Play reviews were not reached. Main primary sources are the **Apple App Store US customer-review RSS feeds** (most recent ~50 reviews each) for Sudoku.com (Easybrain, id 1193508329, confirmed via iTunes Search API), Brainium Sudoku (id 418044512) and Good Sudoku by Zach Gage (id 1489118195), plus one Hacker News thread (2023, older) and one Microsoft Q&A thread (2024, older).
- The App Store review text came through a summarising fetch tool. Quotes below are the excerpts it returned as verbatim; treat them as **near-verbatim** and spot-check them against the live feed before publishing.
- **Sampling bias:** "most recent" feeds overweight current complaints. The Sudoku.com feed (Sept 30–Oct 4 2026) is dominated by reaction to what looks like a recent increase in ad frequency. That means ad frequency is a strong *current* switching trigger. It does not show that ads are the main reason across all players.
- Usernames were removed. App store pages and thread URLs are kept.

## Jobs-to-be-done ranked by evidence strength

1. **Daily habit / routine ("play every day")** — medium. Voices in 3 app audiences (Sudoku.com, Good Sudoku, Brainium), but all from the App Store. Long tenure is common ("3 years", "four iPhone upgrades").
2. **Calm focus / relaxation ("peaceful", "concentration", "flow", "in peace")** — medium. Mostly inferred from *what breaks it* (mid-game interruptions, overstimulating animations). Few positive statements of the job itself. The 2017 HN comment about relaxation is older and was paraphrased by the summariser.
3. **Getting better / mastery (learning techniques)** — medium. Strong but concentrated in Good Sudoku reviews. Supported by HN enthusiasts' demand for logic-only puzzles and pencil tools.
4. **Keep mind sharp / kill time** — low. One explicit voice ("Fun time waster that keeps mind sharp").
5. **Competition (tournaments, leaderboards)** — low/insufficient. Only implied: Good Sudoku users complain when tournament scoring breaks. No one stated it as a reason to play.
6. **Bedtime routine** — not found this run.

## Verdict on N5 (streaks/stats): **partly supported**

- **Supported (skill growth):** players clearly value *visible learning*, e.g. "I'm now half decent" and "learned more techniques". This half of N5 has medium evidence.
- **Supported (records as possessions):** losing history, status, daily completion or achievements causes frustration (MS Sudoku 2024 and Good Sudoku 2026: two apps, two communities). That suggests progress records have value, mostly through loss aversion.
- **Not evidenced:** no one praised streaks or stats as a reason they *stay*. There was no "I love my streak" quote. There was also no direct evidence that streaks are *stressful*. The only stress signal is about reward *animations* ("distracting and overstimulating", "pointless") on Sudoku.com. That warns against loud gamified feedback, not against quiet stats.
- **Implication (inference):** treat progress data as something to protect (sync, offline-safe, never lost), and keep progress feedback quiet and optional. Do not lead with it as a headline feature.

Vendor-style words that players did **not** use in what we retrieved: "brain training", "IQ", "cognitive improvement", "challenge your brain". These come from store listings and listicles (e.g. the alternativeto.net blurbs). Low confidence: the absence may come from the sources we could reach.

## New needs discovered

- **N-new-A: Respectful interruption contract.** No interruptions mid-puzzle. If there are ads, show them only between games. Paid means truly ad-free.
- **N-new-B: Calm, low-stimulation UI.** Optional or no celebration animations, mutable audio, no flashing elements (also an accessibility issue).
- **N-new-C: Offline-first reliability and progress preservation.** Play on a plane, no loading hangs, history and dailies never lost, sync across devices.
- **N-new-D: Input-mode flexibility.** Cell-first and number-first ("number lock"), pencil marks, toggleable highlighting and mistake-checking.
- **N-new-E: Graduated teaching hints for true beginners.** More than one piece of information per hint, with a less crowded UI.
- **N-new-F: Puzzle quality guarantee.** Unique solution, solvable by logic without guessing.
