## Behavior verification

Headless Chrome drove a scratch build through four player journeys via the DevTools protocol, using real clicks and keys. Screenshots and scripts are in the session scratchpad (`behavior/`).

**Journeys, all passed:**

- **Free Play:**
  - mouse, keyboard and Notes entry
  - Undo and Ctrl+Z
  - Restart → Keep going
  - Lamplight
  - Hint: nudge → explain → "What's a naked single?" → show me the move
  - Mistake Hint
- **Album and City:**
  - Paris solved → Postcard reveal (focus on "Add to Album", nav inert, "Solved" announced) → "just arrived", 1/10
  - Viewer: Close, Esc and an outside click all close it
  - Locked slots
  - Mid unlocks after 3 Near
  - "Something doesn't fit yet"
  - A solved grid is inert
- **Replay:** no confirm when untouched → confirm when a replay is in progress → Start fresh → "Solved again. Paris is already in your Album.", count unchanged.
- **Switching:** Lisbon ↔ Free Play both resume exactly, including Notes.

**Other observations:**

- No console errors anywhere.
- A reload loses everything. This is expected until Epic 4.
- The run surfaced **D2**: a low-contrast caption, now measured.
- Undo is empty after switching Sessions. This is by design (`openSession`), but pressing Undo after a switch does nothing silently.
- Undo, Hint and Restart stay visible but inert on a solved puzzle.

**Not exercised:**

- number-first input
- Assist toggles
- Restart → Restart
- the Free Play replace confirm (D1's path)
- Medium/Hard generation and failure
- Far unlocks
- Edge, Firefox and Safari
